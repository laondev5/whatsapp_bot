# Sendbit Service Information

## Overview
Sendbit is a digital payment platform designed for freelancers, bloggers, and professionals, particularly across Africa, to handle global financial transactions seamlessly. It is trusted by over 500k users.

## Key Features
- **PayPal Invoice System**: Create and send PayPal invoices to clients worldwide and receive payments directly.
- **USD Virtual Debit Cards**:
  - **Cost**: $1.5 to create a card.
  - **Min Deposit**: $3 required to start transacting.
  - **Limits**: Ranges from $1,000 to $10,000 depending on the card type.
  - **Usage**: Suitable for international online purchases, subscriptions, and bill payments.
- **Global Multi-Currency Accounts**: Access international bank accounts in **USD, GBP, and EUR**.
- **Crypto Payments**:
  - Securely buy, sell, and transact with major cryptocurrencies including **Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and USDT**.
  - Real-time processing and low-cost transfers.
- **Bulk Services & Bill Payments**: 
  - Purchase airtime and data bundles.
  - Pay utility bills (especially focused on the Nigerian market).
- **Gift Card Trading**: Trade various gift cards on the platform.

## Getting Started
1. **Create an Account**: Provide a username, legal first and last name, phone number, and a secure password.
2. **Verify Identity**: Complete the verification process, which includes receiving an OTP via the registered phone number.
3. **Start Transacting**: Access the dashboard to generate invoices, request virtual cards, or manage crypto wallets.

## How to Create a PayPal Invoice
1. Log in to your Sendbit account.
2. Navigate to the **PayPal Services** section.
3. Fill in the invoice details, including the amount and description.
4. Send the invoice link to your client for payment.

## Security & Reliability
- **Multi-layer Encryption**: Ensures data privacy and transaction security.
- **Priority Support**: Dedicated customer service team available via the Help Center.
- **Fraud Prevention**: Built-in mechanisms to handle disputes and fraudulent activities.
